Workouts and sounds

The ready-made workouts follow well-known interval formats, such as the 20 seconds on and 10 off described by Izumi Tabata's research group, the four-minute intervals used in Norwegian studies and the fartlek sessions associated with Swedish coaches. The formats are common knowledge among coaches. The descriptions in the app are the developer's own.

Every beep, bell, chime and whistle in Redline is generated inside the app from scratch. No recordings or samples are used, so there is nothing to credit. A sound you choose yourself is your own file and remains your responsibility.
