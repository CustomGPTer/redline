No adverts and nothing to unlock in Redline

Redline has no paid version, no subscription and no adverts. Well-known interval timers on Google Play are free to install and pay their way with adverts, a paid upgrade or both.

Download for Android

Free. Android 10 or later. What it does

Where your training is kept

Redline keeps your workouts, your history and your heart-rate records on your phone and runs a workout with or without an internet connection. It has no account and no servers. If that matters to you, check each app's privacy policy and its Data safety section on Google Play before you install it.

What Redline does not do

  • It is not on Google Play, so it is installed from a downloaded file and updated by installing the new version over the old one.
  • It has no watch app. Eugene Sharafan's listing says Tabata Timer works with Wear OS watches.
  • It does not measure heart rate with the phone or from a smartwatch. It needs a Bluetooth chest strap or armband.
  • It has no pictures or videos of exercises and does not teach technique. Exercise names are labels for the timer. Tabata Timer's listing says pictures can be added to intervals.
  • It has no metronome and no repetition tempo mode, both of which Tabata Timer's listing describes.
  • It cannot give each block its own music, which Runloop's listing describes for Seconds.
  • The voice is your phone's built-in voice, not a recorded announcer, so it sounds the way your phone sounds.
  • The TV clock works through screen mirroring or a cable. It does not cast straight to a Chromecast.
  • Reminders can arrive a few minutes late when the phone is saving battery.
  • Health Connect needs Android 14, or the Health Connect app on older phones.
  • The app is in English only.
