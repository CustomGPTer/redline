Redline 1.0 for Android. First release.

Redline is an interval timer for HIIT, Tabata, circuits and running intervals. Pick a workout or build your own, press Start, and it fills the screen with the colour of each block, beeps and speaks every change and keeps going with the screen off.

**Added**
- 36 ready-made workouts, including Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP, the Norwegian 4x4, fartleks, circuits and boxing rounds.
- Your own workouts, built by rounds or block by block, or typed as one line such as `8x(20/10)`. Quick Start and Surprise me.
- A full-screen timer with a colour for each block, giant digits and a ring that empties. Gym display, and a full-screen clock on a mirrored TV.
- Runs with the screen off, with Pause, Skip and Finish in the notification and an optional lock-screen display.
- Five beep sets, your own sound for Go, spoken cues in the phone's voice, vibration and a screen flash. Music dips for cues.
- Bluetooth heart-rate straps and armbands: live rate, five zones, target-zone coaching and calories.
- History calendar, session pages with graphs, a weekly goal, streaks, records, trends and 22 badges.
- Five multi-week plans, more than 160 exercise names, and workout reminders.
- Sharing by QR code, code or file. Health Connect, two widgets, icon shortcuts and backup to a file.

Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/redline/releases.html

Android 10 or later. Download `Redline.apk` below and open it to install.
