Version 1.0

First release

LatestReleased 17 November 2026

Added

  • 36 ready-made workouts in eight folders: Getting started, Classics, Circuits, Sprints, Endurance, Walking and running, Combat, and EMOM and AMRAP sessions.
  • Your own workouts, built by rounds (warm-up, work, rest, rounds, sets, a break between sets, cool-down, rest first, skip the last rest) or block by block.
  • Type it: a workout written as one line of text, such as 8x(20/10).
  • Quick Start, and Surprise me, which makes a session from a length, a level and a style.
  • Favourites, folders, search and duplicate.
  • More than 160 exercise names in eight groups, or type your own.
  • A full-screen timer with a colour for each kind of block, giant digits and a ring that empties.
  • Pause, skip, back and finish, a tap-lock, and a get-ready countdown.
  • Workouts carry on with the screen off, with the countdown and Pause, Skip and Finish in the notification. A switch shows the timer over the lock screen, and another keeps the screen on.
  • Gym display: sideways, full brightness, the largest digits.
  • A full-screen clock on a TV or monitor when the phone is mirrored or connected by cable.
  • A tap counter for AMRAP workouts.
  • Five beep sets (Classic, Ringside bell, Digital, Soft chime and Whistle) and your own sound for Go.
  • Spoken cues in the phone's own voice: each change, round numbers, exercise names, halfway, the last seconds and the time left. The phrases can be changed.
  • Vibration and a screen flash at each change.
  • Music from another app dips for cues, or is left alone. Play, pause and skip buttons on the timer.
  • Sound, voice and vibration settings that can be different for one workout.
  • Bluetooth heart-rate chest straps and armbands: live rate, zone, lowest, average and highest, and a live graph.
  • Five heart-rate zones from your maximum, which comes from your age, your own figure or your highest reading.
  • Target zones for a workout, with the voice saying when to push harder or ease off.
  • Calories from heart rate, weight and age, or an estimate when there is no sensor.
  • History: a month calendar and a page for each session with its graph, time in zones, beats recovered, an effort score from 1 to 10 and notes.
  • Progress: a weekly goal, day and weeks-on-goal streaks, planned rest days, records, eight-week trends, a heat-map of the year and 22 badges.
  • Five plans: First six weeks, Road to Tabata, Build to 4x4, Walk to run and Engine builder.
  • Sharing a workout by QR code, as a short code to paste or as a file, and scanning, pasting or opening one sent to you.
  • A reminder for any workout, on the days and at the time you choose.
  • Health Connect: finished workouts written with heart rate and calories. Off until switched on.
  • Two home-screen widgets, Start a workout and Weekly goal, and icon shortcuts for Quick Start, Last workout and Surprise me.
  • Backup to a file and restore from one.
  • A safety notice on first use, with the full terms in Settings, and a short guide to interval training.
  • A notice on the home screen when a newer version is out.

Installing and updating

  • When you download, Chrome may warn that the file might be harmful. It shows this warning for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway.
  • Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser or file manager to install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.
  • Google Play Protect may say it does not recognise the app, or block it, because Redline does not come from Google Play. Choose More details, then Install anyway.
  • The first time you start a workout, allow notifications so that the countdown can show while the screen is off. The timer runs either way.
  • If you pair a heart-rate sensor, Android asks you to allow Redline to find nearby devices. That is Bluetooth, and it is used only for the sensor.
  • To update, download the latest version and install it over the one you have. Your workouts, history and settings are kept.
  • Every version is signed with the same key, so Android accepts an update only if it comes from the same developer.

Known limits

  • Some phones stop background apps to save battery. If a workout stops when the screen goes off, open the phone's settings for Redline, choose Battery and allow it to run without restriction.
  • Reminders can arrive a few minutes late when the phone is in deep battery saving.
  • Health Connect needs Android 14, or the Health Connect app on older phones.
  • The TV clock needs screen mirroring or a cable. It does not cast straight to a Chromecast.
